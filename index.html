    Plan your day at USS

    A miniature of the whole park, built to show how short the walks actually are — and where the queues aren't.

    Getting around

    • Drag to orbit, right-drag to pan, scroll to zoom
    • Click any ride or label for details
    • Pick a zone to fly there — the park is one loop around a lagoon
    • T top-down plan · R reset the camera

    Filters that matter

    • Rider height greys out anything that would turn you away — set it once for the shortest person in your group
    • Filter by thrill rides, family rides, shows and food
    • Walk rings show 100 m and 200 m from whatever you've selected

    Building a plan

    • + adds a ride to your plan (saved in this browser)
    • Optimise reorders stops to cut the walking
    • The plan totals walking, queueing and riding for a realistic day length
    • Queue estimates follow the park time slider — drag it and watch the numbers move
    • Tour it flies your own plan in order

    Keys

    • F flight mode · Space pause
    • L labels · M miniature blur · N night
    • 1–7 jump to a zone · Esc close
    Reality check: this is a hand-built stylised model, not a survey. Positions follow the park's real loop layout and the walking distances are close, but building shapes are interpretations. Universal Studios Singapore changes often — Minion Land replaced the Madagascar zone in 2025 — and height limits, wait times, Express availability and show schedules all shift. Check the official USS app on the day. Wait times here are modelled typical patterns, not live data.
